## Good analysis

- **Documented:** model/date, brochure promise, notice and trial offer.
- **Owner report:** prior use without recurring payment, unless usage evidence supports it.
- **Unknown:** accepted Cloud terms, exact contractual duration, actual restriction and relevant provider's clause.
- **Not established:** that the system stopped working, that all Airzone users are affected, that the policy is unlawful, or that the whole installation price must be refunded.
- **Coverage:** do not import Aidoo-specific exceptions into a Webserver case.
- **Recipient:** identify seller and Cloud provider separately. The invoice alone does not establish which entity can answer the Cloud contract question.
