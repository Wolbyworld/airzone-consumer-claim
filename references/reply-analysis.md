# Analyze a company response

Read the entire reply and attachments. Preserve dates, sender entity and original question. Summarize fairly before challenging it.

| Topic | Owner asked | Company actually answered | Proof/citation | Remaining precise question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coverage | Does this affect [model/date]? | [Answer] | [E07 section] | [Only if still unresolved] |
| Function | Will [specific remote function] remain free? | [Answer] | [E07] | [Scope, duration, conditions] |
| Contract | What accepted clause allows the change? | [Answer] | [E03/E07] | Version/date/clause and acceptance evidence |
| Remedy | Preserve previously included function | [Offer] | [E07] | Is this permanent or trial-only? |
| Charges | Was a charge actually taken? | [Answer] | [Evidence] | Amount/date/authorization if relevant |

Common responses and useful follow-up:

- **“Cloud costs/security/new features”**: acknowledge the rationale. Ask how it supports changing this owner's original bargain; do not dispute that running a service costs money.
- **“Six free months”**: distinguish a temporary offer from continued access after the trial. Ask what happens when it ends. Do not activate it automatically.
- **“Local control remains”**: accept that fact if accurate; identify the specific outside-home function that differs. Do not claim the whole system is unusable.
- **“Terms allow changes”**: request the exact relevant clause, version, acceptance record, notice and reason; assess scope and fairness rather than declaring all change clauses invalid.
- **“Your model is exempt”**: seek written device/date-specific confirmation and verify it. A favorable response may resolve the case; do not escalate just to generate volume.
- **Concrete refund/alternative**: compare against the owner's objective, costs, conditions and release language. No automatic acceptance or withdrawal.
- **No response**: verify delivery and applicable complaint rules before using non-response as a legal trigger. An administrative or requested courtesy deadline is not interchangeable with a statutory one.

Recommend one targeted follow-up or the appropriate next forum, not repeated near-identical emails. Preserve any genuine admission, partial concession and unresolved issue. Use `assets/reply-followup-es.md` only after this analysis.
