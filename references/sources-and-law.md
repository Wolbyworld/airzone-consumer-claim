# Official sources and legal routing

Research baseline: **29 October 2026**. Recheck at the time of every claim or filing. Product coverage, prices, procedures and law can change. Record current URL, relevant wording, retrieval date and why it applies to this owner. This file is a research map, not proof of their contract.

## Airzone: current policy, not an individual entitlement

The following official pages were inspected in the live browser on 29 October 2026; their displayed update date was 28 May 2026. Text-only retrieval can return an empty JavaScript shell: use a supported browser to read the rendered page, or ask for the owner's copy. Do not claim a blank response means the policy does not exist.

- [Plan conditions](https://support.airzonecloud.com/#/support/PLAN_CLOUD/content/CLOUD_PLAN_CONDITIONS): states launch on 28 October 2026, €48/year including taxes **per site**, and six free months **from activation**. - [Feature table](https://support.airzonecloud.com/#/support/PLAN_CLOUD/content/CLOUD_PLAN_FUNCTIONS): local control is listed without subscription; remote control, remote schedules and cloud/voice integrations are listed under the paid plan, subject to Aidoo-specific conditions.

It says basic remote control remains subscription-free for Aidoo Wi-Fi and for Pro bought before launch; Wi-Fi 2.0 has the subscription model. These statements describe published policy. They do not establish that any particular device has already lost a function or that a subscription is legally enforceable for that owner.

## Spain: issue map, not automatic conclusions

[TRLGDCU — RDL 1/2007, consolidated BOE](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-20555)

Check consumer status (art. 3), pre-contract information and incorporation of advertising (arts. 60–61), good-faith integration (65), transparent/unfair terms (80, 82, 85), and applicable conformity/digital-service provisions (114 onward). Applicability depends on the purchase/contract dates, object and supply arrangement; a hardware purchase and separate Cloud service may need distinct analysis.

Arts. 126–126 bis address certain digital-service changes, with conditions and potential termination rights. Their transitional application matters: the BOE notes application to contracts concluded from 1 January 2022. Do not automatically apply them to older contracts, assume every change is unlawful, or treat termination as a guaranteed right to lifetime free service or a full installation refund. Check any notification-linked time limit promptly with qualified help.

### Pre-court requirement

[LO 1/2025, additional provision 7](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2025-76#da-7) provides a consumer-specific route for the pre-litigation requirement through a prior complaint to the contracting business where the response is absent within the applicable period or unsatisfactory. Preserve complaint, delivery and response. Counsel should verify the eventual defendant, cause of action, timing, exceptions and any confidentiality implications. A complaint to the manufacturer is not necessarily sufficient for a claim against a different seller.

### Official routes

- [Comunidad de Madrid consumer complaint procedure](https://sede.comunidad.madrid/denuncias-reclamaciones-recursos/reclamaciones-consumo): verify competence, current form, eligibility, identity/representation, documents and notification options. Use the local OMIC/other region's official procedure where appropriate.
- [Madrid: what happens after a complaint](https://www.comunidad.madrid/sites/default/files/doc/consumo/que_sucede_tras_reclamar_resp_v1.pdf): mediation seeks a solution; possible inspection/transfer/archive does not itself establish compensation.
- [Madrid consumer arbitration](https://www.comunidad.madrid/consumo/arbitraje-consumo-0): generally free and voluntary, requiring a valid applicable commitment or company acceptance. The award is binding; it is not a rehearsal before retrying the same merits in court. The published timetable is generally 90 calendar days from formal commencement, potentially extended by up to 90 for complexity. Verify prerequisites/admissibility and dates; a submitted request is not formal commencement.
- [LEC, current consolidated BOE PDF](https://boe.es/buscar/pdf/2000/BOE-A-2000-323-consolidado.pdf): verify procedure, jurisdiction, valuation, evidence, representation and costs (including arts. 23/31). Do not advertise a blanket “under €2,000 = no lawyer” route: preserving ongoing functionality may not be a simple quantified money claim. Get proportionate consumer-law advice before court.
- [OCU consumer complaint information](https://www.ocu.org/reclamar/informacion-consumidores): read current service terms, visibility, privacy, membership and channel rules. OCU is not the administration or a court. A report does not guarantee representation, a collective action, compensation or an official file.

## Outside Spain

Stop applying the Spanish legal templates as law. Identify governing jurisdiction and the competent local consumer authority/ADR route using current official sources. Translate the factual structure, not unverified legal conclusions. Cross-border, business purchases, complex contractual chains and imminent limitation periods merit qualified advice.

## Local alternatives are a separate optional task

If the owner asks about mitigation, start with [Airzone's local API documentation](https://developers.airzonecloud.com/docs/local-api/#overview) and verify hardware/firmware support. Do not promise a VPN/Home Assistant/local API works on every model, expose a controller to the internet, alter the installation or characterize a workaround as settlement. Preserve evidence first; technical changes need separate authorization.
