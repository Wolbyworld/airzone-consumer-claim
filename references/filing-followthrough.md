# Filing and following through

## Select the route, not every route

1. **Company complaint**: clear facts, remedy, relevant evidence; correct legal entity and verifiable delivery.
2. **Consumer mediation/intermediation**: usually the proportionate next step after an unresolved reply, using the competent OMIC or regional authority. Check its current prerequisite rules. Prefer one existing file; supplement it rather than filing the same matter repeatedly.
3. **Optional OCU pattern report**: separate, minimized and owner-approved. See campaign guidance. It does not replace a formal complaint or pause legal deadlines by assumption.
4. **Arbitration**: only after explaining binding consequences and checking company coverage/acceptance, jurisdiction, subject matter and current rules. Never preselect the arbitration box as a harmless escalation.
5. **Civil claim**: costed decision with qualified help when appropriate, including exact defendant(s), remedy, valuation, limitation and pre-litigation requirements. Do not promise “free court” or a guaranteed win.

Inspection/sanction and individual redress are different objectives. A sanction request is not a shortcut to compensation. A failed mediation is not an adverse judicial ruling.

## Madrid procedure snapshot — reverify before acting

The official procedure checked 29 October 2026 is fee-free, asks for identity/contracting status, respondent, facts, proof and remedy, and supports digital or eligible in-person routes. Digital submission requires a recognized signing system. A saved draft or downloaded form is not a filing. The portal distinguishes arbitration from ordinary intermediation. It currently describes **one month from receipt** to make observations on the company's response and an **average** processing time of **three months**, not a guaranteed decision deadline. Use the actual notice and current rules to calculate deadlines. [Official procedure](https://sede.comunidad.madrid/denuncias-reclamaciones-recursos/reclamaciones-consumo)

## Pre-submit gate

- Verify this is not already filed. Read existing receipt, portal record and ledger; do not rely only on a task marked “done”.
- Confirm the competent office and exact procedure. Use verified official links, not search-ad lookalikes.
- Confirm claimant/representation, respondent legal entity, desired remedy, chronology and file list. Do not automatically use a brand name or seller address for the Cloud provider.
- Resolve all placeholders. Validate attachment sizes/formats, legibility and cross-references. Include full relevant reply, not only unfavorable excerpts.
- Review required identity disclosures privately. No identity document in public campaign material. Do not request private keys/passwords/OTPs in chat.
- Show the final text, destination, attachments, selected route and notification preferences to the owner. Record explicit approval; obtain separate informed approval for arbitration or settlement.
- Hand off authentication/certificate/signature/declaration steps where required. State exactly what the owner must do and what evidence to return. Do not say the claim was filed while waiting.

## Submission proof and uncertain outcomes

A reliable completion record should include registration/reference number, date/time, recipient office, submitted document or attachment list, and the receipt file or portal confirmation. Check that the proof corresponds to this claimant, matter and route. Preserve it privately; public summaries should not reveal verification codes/QRs.

If the browser closes, signature fails or final submit times out: inspect drafts, “Mis Gestiones”, notification/email confirmation and existing receipts before any retry. If no conclusive evidence exists, mark **submission unconfirmed** and ask for the missing confirmation; don't create another filing to feel finished. Retry only after establishing no accepted submission or receiving official guidance.

## Monitoring and completion

Record next trigger, owner and source, not a guessed daily deadline. Monitor only if requested and authorized, using the existing channel/file. The arrival of a company reply triggers analysis and any response window. An official request for documents takes priority; verify its receipt date and exact deadline. A pending or unchanged status is not resolved.

When a remedy is offered, check duration, device/function coverage, costs, implementation and waiver/withdrawal terms. The owner decides acceptance. If accepted, verify actual delivery before closing. Retain proof according to the owner's needs and applicable obligations; do not delete originals or withdraw a case automatically.

Report status plainly: **prepared**, **awaiting owner signature**, **sent**, **registered**, **awaiting company/authority**, **deadline requiring action**, or **resolved by [verified outcome]**. Do not call registration an admission on the merits, or an acknowledgment a victory.
