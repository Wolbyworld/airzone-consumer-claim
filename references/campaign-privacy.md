# Factual visibility and opt-in coordination

Use this only when the owner asks for wider visibility. Preparation is not permission to publish, contact journalists, email strangers, launch a website, collect supporters or share the skill. Deliver drafts first. No affiliation with Airzone, OCU, a regulator or a law firm is implied.

## Campaign standard

A useful campaign makes it easier for real owners to document their own situation and ask for a concrete solution. Focus on preserving previously included functions, clear model-specific terms and proportionate redress. Distinguish published company policy, a verified individual experience and a legal allegation. A current subscription price is not evidence that every owner was charged.

Avoid “scam”, “theft”, “extortion”, “illegal for everyone” and unsupported totals. Do not claim a collective action, investigation, victory or thousands affected without current verifiable evidence. Link the company's primary explanation and exceptions. Correct inaccurate claims promptly; record the correction rather than quietly spreading it again.

No fake complaints, multiple identities, bulk templated filings, employee targeting, scraped owner lists, bought engagement or coordinated ratings manipulation. People may describe their genuine experience in their own words through normal channels. Do not pressure them to post a rating or disclose private evidence publicly.

## Privacy-preserving supporter intake — proposal only

Before deploying any form, obtain separate approval and decide who operates it, purpose, lawful basis, access, retention, security, withdrawal/deletion contact and whether data will be shared. Verify applicable privacy requirements with an official source or qualified help. This checklist is not a ready-made GDPR compliance notice.

Prefer **no centralized personal-document collection**. Give owners a local evidence checklist and official filing links. If an opt-in contact list is justified, collect at most contact email, country/region, model family, approximate purchase period, affected function and optional status. Avoid DNI, full address, serials, invoice/claim numbers, passwords and copies of invoices. Do not require public posting to participate.

Keep consent choices separate: receive updates; be contacted privately by a named organizer; share an anonymized summary; speak to a named journalist. No prechecked boxes and no bundled consent to unknown recipients. Withdrawal from updates is not withdrawal of a legal complaint. Explain any non-deletion obligations before collection.

Public metrics must disclose collection method, date range, sample size, self-reported versus verified status and deduplication method. An opt-in sample is not a prevalence estimate. Small combinations of region/model/date can identify people: aggregate or suppress them. Keep a separate consent record; do not publish supporter contact details.

## OCU or journalist outreach

Resolve the named recipient/channel and verify current contact/portal rules. OCU may offer public and private visibility options: inspect before submission. Use the minimum facts needed; remove raw receipts and identity documents. A journalist does not need an owner's DNI or registration verification code to evaluate a story. Offer a redacted dossier only after approval; additional recipient or publicity permission is a separate decision.

For an already reported case, send a concise approved update using its existing reference rather than a duplicate complaint. Explain what is new and what action is requested. Do not characterize OCU involvement as representation without confirmation.

Use [campaign-kit-es.md](../assets/campaign-kit-es.md) for a one-page explainer, opt-in invitation and targeted outreach draft. All remain private review copies until explicitly approved.

## Public-release gate for source materials

Do not publish or include raw private emails, invoices, correspondence, receipts, screenshots or third-party materials in the public repository or campaign, even if someone says they have been anonymized. Before any proposed excerpt or derivative is shared, separately review personal-data risk, confidentiality, permission/reproduction rights and contextual accuracy, and obtain the owner's explicit approval for that exact public material. Redaction alone does not establish permission to republish. Prefer original neutral summaries and links to official public sources; never copy a private case dossier into the reusable kit.

This repository is documentation only. Do not add forms, file uploads, analytics, trackers or an owner-case intake service as part of publishing it. Those would require a new authorized scope and privacy/security review.
